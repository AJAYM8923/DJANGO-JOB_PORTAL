# 🏢 Job Portal with Admin Verification & Resume Management

A **role-based job portal** that connects verified employers with job seekers, ensuring authenticity and efficiency in recruitment.  
Built with **Python (Django)** and **PostgreSQL**, featuring resume handling, job posting approvals, and smart search.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ✅ Current Features
- **Role-Based Access**  
  - **Admin** – Approves employers and job postings before they go live.  
  - **Employer** – Can post jobs (pending admin approval) and view applicants.  
  - **Job Seeker** – Can apply for jobs with an uploaded resume.
  
- **Job Posting Approval** – Jobs appear publicly only after admin verification.  
- **Resume Management** – Job seekers upload resumes; employers can view & download them from their dashboard.  
- **Employer Dashboard** – Displays applicants for each job with direct resume access.  
- **Job Search** – Filter jobs by title and other criteria for quick navigation.

---

### 🤖 Upcoming AI-Powered Features
- **AI Job Matching** – Automatically match candidates to the most relevant jobs.  
- **Resume Keyword Optimization** – Suggest improvements to beat ATS filters.  
- **Intelligent Recommendations** – Jobs find candidates before they search.  
- **Candidate Ranking** – AI ranks applicants for employers based on skills & fit.
